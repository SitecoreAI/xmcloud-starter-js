About Allianz Life

  • Our name is pronounced "AH-Lee-Ahnz."
  • We’ve been helping Americans prepare for their financial futures since 1896.
  • We are headquartered in Minneapolis.
  • Allianz Life has about 2,000 employees in the United States. The majority work in Minnesota.
  • We are recognized among the nation’s top employers with awards including FORTUNE 100 Best Companies to Work For, Star Tribune’s Top Workplaces, and Ethisphere World’s Most Ethical Companies.1
  • We are a subsidiary of Allianz SE, one of the world’s largest financial services companies. Allianz SE is based in Munich, Germany and serves more than 97 million2 customers worldwide.
