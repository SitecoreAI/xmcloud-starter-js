For more complete information about Allianz Life of NY variable annuities and any available variable options, call your financial professional or Allianz Life Financial Services, LLC, member FINRA, at 800.624.0197 for a prospectus. The prospectuses contain details on investment objectives, risks, fees, and expenses, as well as other information about the RILA and the variable options, which you should carefully consider. Please read the prospectuses thoroughly before sending money.
