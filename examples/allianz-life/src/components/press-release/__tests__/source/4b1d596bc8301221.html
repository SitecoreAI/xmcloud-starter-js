Allianz Life Recognized as a Top Workplace for LGBTQ+ Employees

Company receives perfect score from Human Rights Campaign’s Corporate Equality Index.

MINNEAPOLIS – Jan. 7, 2025 – Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America (Allianz Life) received a perfect score of 100 out of 100 on the Human Rights Campaign Foundation’s (HRCF) 2025 Corporate Equality Index, the nation’s foremost benchmarking survey and report measuring corporate policies and practices related to LGBTQ+ workplace equality. Allianz Life is one of the 765 U.S. businesses that will be honored with HRCF’s Equality 100 Award as Leaders in LGBTQ+ Workplace Inclusion.

“Allianz Life takes pride in continually being a great place to work,” said Jenny Guldseth, chief people and culture officer, Allianz Life. “We have worked hard to build a caring, inclusive and equitable culture that values the diversity of all employees’ perspectives, lived experiences and talent, which is reflected in our perfect score on the Corporate Equality Index.”

Criteria for this index serve as a roadmap for how major U.S. businesses promote inclusive policies, practices and benefits for LGBTQ+ employees to advance workplace equity, including:

  • Non-discrimination policies across business entities
  • Equitable benefits for LGBTQ+ employees and their families
  • Supporting an inclusive culture
  • Corporate social responsibility

The full report is available online at www.hrc.org/cei, and you can learn more about diversity, equity and inclusion at Allianz Life at allianzlife.com/diversity.

About Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America
