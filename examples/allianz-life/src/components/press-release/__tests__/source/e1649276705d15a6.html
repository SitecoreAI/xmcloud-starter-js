AllianzIM to Transfer Exchange Listings and Fund Name Changes

AllianzIM today announces the listing exchange for multiple Buffered ETFs will transfer from the NYSE Arca to Cboe BZX Exchange effective on or about Dec. 22, 2025.

MINNEAPOLIS – Dec. 5, 2025 – Allianz Investment Management LLC (AllianzIM), a subsidiary of Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America and adviser to the AllianzIM exchange-traded funds (ETFs), today announces the listing exchange for multiple Buffered ETFs will transfer from the NYSE Arca to Cboe BZX Exchange effective on or about Dec. 22, 2025.

In addition, the firm also will be updating the naming mechanism for the U.S. Large Cap Buffer10 (for both the six-month and one-year outcome periods), the U.S. Large Cap Buffer20 products, effective on or about Dec. 22, 2025.

Current shareholders do not need to take any action, and the funds’ investment objectives, strategies, and portfolio management teams will remain the same. For more information on the full AllianzIM Buffered ETF product lineup, visit https://www.allianzim.com/product-table/.

Built to help mitigate risk and lower volatility, AllianzIM’s Buffered Outcome ETFs allow investors to participate in the growth potential of the equity markets up to a stated cap with an explicit downside buffer.
