Allianz Life Recognized on the 2025 Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For® List

Company recognized for culture built on outstanding benefits, work/life balance, and employee growth

MINNEAPOLIS – April 2, 2025 – Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America (Allianz Life) has again been named one of the 2025 Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For®. This is the 13th time the company has been included on the list, and builds on the company’s certification as a Great Place to Work in 2024. This year, Allianz Life ranked number 87 on the list, jumping 11 spots from 2024. 

“Our employees are at the heart of our success,” said Jasmine Jirele, president and CEO, Allianz Life. “We are committed to ensuring everyone feels empowered to prioritize their well-being and personal growth, both of which play an important role in helping to make Allianz Life an employer of choice.”

The 100 Best Companies to Work For list honors companies that build a high-trust workplace environment, measured through Great Place To Work’s proprietary survey platform. Companies are assessed on their ability to create a great employee experience that cuts across job level, business unit, race, gender, age, disability status, or any aspect of employee identity. Inclusion on the Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For list means Allianz Life has been selected as one of the top employers in the U.S. based on rigorous analytics and confidential employee feedback from more than 1.3 million U.S. employees.

Allianz Life understands that when employees know they have the time and resources to care for themselves first, they can then better care for customers. That is why in addition to continuous learning and development, global rotational opportunities, and an extensive employee recognition program, Allianz Life offers an array of benefits and on-campus amenities that help employees find work/life balance, including:

  • Hybrid and flexible work arrangements
  • Work-from-anywhere-for-a-month policy
  • Fully paid, one-month sabbatical to volunteer full time
  • 7.5% 401(k) company match
  • Onsite health clinic with a nurse practitioner and mental health provider
  • Family and adoption leave for all families
  • Adoption and Surrogacy Assistance Reimbursement Program
  • Onsite childcare center
  • Free onsite fitness center
  • Lifestyle spending account
  • Tuition reimbursement
  • Employee assistance program

This award builds on Allianz Life’s ongoing dedication to company culture, work/life balance, and continuous learning. Other recent top workplace recognitions include:

  • Being named one of the 2025 World’s Most Ethical Companies® by Ethisphere for the sixth consecutive year.
  • Ascending to the top five on the 2025 APEX Training Awards for Allianz Life’s excellence in training and development programs.
  • Receiving a perfect score from Human Rights Campaign’s 2025 Corporate Equality Index, recognizing Allianz Life as a top workplace for LGBTQ+ employees.
  • Being awarded as a Top Workplace by the Star Tribune for the 15th consecutive year in 2024.

Learn more about what makes Allianz Life a great place to work at www.allianzlife.com/culture. 

About the Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For

Great Place to Work selected the 100 Best list by gathering and analyzing more than 1.3 million confidential survey responses representing the experiences of more than 8.4 million U.S. employees. Of those, over 670,000 responses were received from employees at eligible companies, and this list is based on that feedback. Organizations are assessed on their efforts to create generous, supportive, high-performance work experiences for every employee in the organization. Companies must be Great Place To Work Certified™ with 1,000 or more employees in the U.S. and cannot be a government agency. Read the full methodology.

To be considered, all companies use the Great Place to Work Trust Index™ survey. To get on this list next year, start here.

About Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America, (Allianz Life), one of the Ethisphere World’s Most Ethical Companies®, has been trusted since 1896 to help millions of Americans prepare for financial uncertainties and retirement with a variety of innovative risk management solutions. In 2025, Allianz Life provided additional value to its policyholders via distributions of more than $18.7 billion. Allianz Life is a leading provider of fixed index annuities, registered index-linked annuities, and indexed universal life insurance. Additionally, Allianz Investment Management LLC (AllianzIM), a registered investment adviser and wholly owned subsidiary of Allianz Life,  manages the suite of AllianzIM exchange-traded funds (ETFs). Allianz Life and AllianzIM are part of Allianz SE, a global leader in the financial services industry with more than 156,000 employees in nearly 70 countries. Allianz Life is a proud sponsor of Allianz Field® in St. Paul, Minnesota, home of Major League Soccer’s Minnesota United.
