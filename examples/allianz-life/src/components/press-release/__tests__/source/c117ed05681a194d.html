AllianzIM Grows International Buffered ETF Lineup with Launch of JULI

AllianzIM International Equity Buffer15 Uncapped Jul ETF expands defined downside protection and unlimited upside potential beyond a spread to investors seeking international equity exposure

MINNEAPOLIS – July 1, 2026 – Allianz Investment Management LLC (AllianzIM), a subsidiary of Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America (Allianz Life) and adviser to the AllianzIM exchange-traded funds (ETFs), today announced the expansion of its Buffered ETF lineup with the launch of the AllianzIM International Equity Buffer15 Uncapped Jul ETF (JULI), a new strategy that seeks to provide investors more ways to stay invested while managing downside risk.

As the latest addition to the AllianzIM Uncapped Buffered suite, JULI offers broad international equity and asset exposure via the iShares® MSCI EAFE ETF (EFA) and seeks to reduce the impact of market drawdowns with a 15% downside buffer with uncapped upside beyond a spread over a one-year outcome period.

“As markets have rebounded from a turbulent first quarter, the temptation to move to the sidelines remains real. Elevated inflation, Fed leadership shifts, and ongoing geopolitical uncertainty continue to drive investor anxiety even as indices hit new highs,” said Charles Champagne, Head of ETF Strategy at AllianzIM. “JULI gives investors a way to stay in international equities through that uncertainty, with built-in downside protection and no ceiling on how much they can gain.”

“Investors today are navigating a more complex environment than they’ve faced in years, and our focus is on building strategies that help them stay the course,” said Chris Chambs, CEO of AllianzIM. “JULI continues our commitment to giving investors access to international equity growth with the kind of risk management framework that has always been at the heart of what we do.”

JULI utilizes the core strengths of AllianzIM, which include risk management experience and in-house portfolio management capabilities. As part of one of the largest asset management and diversified insurance companies in the world (Allianz SE), AllianzIM leverages capabilities  from the proprietary hedging platform that is used among affiliates to help manage more than $165.8 billion (as of 12/31/25) in hedged assets for institutional and retail investors around the globe. Offering a new way to help investors mitigate risk and reduce volatility, this ETF advised by affiliate AllianzIM complements the Allianz Life suite of annuity and life insurance products.

FLEX Options are customized equity or index options contracts that trade on an exchange, but provide investors with the ability to customize key contract terms like exercise prices, styles, and expiration dates. An options contract is an agreement between a buyer and seller that gives the purchaser of the option the right, but not the obligation, to buy (in the case of a call option), or to sell (in the case of a put option), a particular asset at a specified future date at an agreed upon price (commonly known as the “strike price”).
