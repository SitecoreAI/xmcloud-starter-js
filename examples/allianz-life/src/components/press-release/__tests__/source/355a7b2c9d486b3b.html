Allianz Life Named Seramount Top Company for Executive Women

Award recognizes company commitment to gender equality.

MINNEAPOLIS – Sept. 10, 2024 – Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America (Allianz Life) has been named as a Seramount 2024 Top 75 Companies for Executive Women. The annual benchmark recognizes companies that promote gender diversity at the executive leadership level through succession planning, career development, equal pay, and other initiatives.

According to Seramount’s latest benchmark data, companies are making progress in advancing gender equality at the senior leadership level. In 2024, companies who participated in the benchmark reported that 37% of all corporate executives identify as women, up from 35% in 2023. This trend extends through promotions, with 44% of promotions to corporate executives identifying as women, up from 47% in 2023.

At Allianz Life, the senior executive team includes four females, or nearly half of its nine members, led by President and CEO Jasmine Jirele. Women also hold 45.5% of the people management roles across the company.

“We embed diversity and gender equality into everything we do,” said Jirele. “The value of diverse leadership across all dimensions fuels innovation, deepens employee engagement, and positions us for future success.”

The annual Seramount benchmark uses an in-depth application process with 200+ questions on topics related to female representation at all levels of the organization but with a primary focus on executive leadership. It closely analyzes each company’s programs and initiatives from skill development and mentoring to succession planning and workplace flexibility. To apply, a company must have at least two female board members and 500 U.S.-based employees.  

To learn more about what it’s like to work at Allianz Life and see current job openings, visit www.allianzlife.com/culture.

About Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America, (Allianz Life), one of the Ethisphere World’s Most Ethical Companies®, has been trusted since 1896 to help millions of Americans prepare for financial uncertainties and retirement with a variety of innovative risk management solutions. In 2025, Allianz Life provided additional value to its policyholders via distributions of more than $18.7 billion. Allianz Life is a leading provider of fixed index annuities, registered index-linked annuities, and indexed universal life insurance. Additionally, Allianz Investment Management LLC (AllianzIM), a registered investment adviser and wholly owned subsidiary of Allianz Life,  manages the suite of AllianzIM exchange-traded funds (ETFs). Allianz Life and AllianzIM are part of Allianz SE, a global leader in the financial services industry with more than 156,000 employees in nearly 70 countries. Allianz Life is a proud sponsor of Allianz Field® in St. Paul, Minnesota, home of Major League Soccer’s Minnesota United.
