Focused on:

Education, certifications, and memberships:

  • Chair of the Minnesota Insurance and Financial Services Council
  • Board member of the Minnesota Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association
  • Board member of the Indiana Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association
