Luca Gallo

Chief Operating Officer
Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Background:

With more than two decades of industry experience, Gallo is the Chief Operating Officer of Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America (Allianz Life). He is responsible for driving the transformation of Allianz Life’s operational and technology processes to support the company’s growth. He has broad experience in operations, technology, and leadership.

Experience:

  • Gallo spent more than 17 years in the Life and Annuities industry in Italy.
  • Previously he was Chief Information Officer for Allianz Worldwide Partners Brazil and Regional CIO for Allianz Partners Americas.
  • He went on to hold various senior-level positions with Allianz Technology of America, including Global Head of Life and Health.
  • Most recently, Gallo was Senior Vice President, Technology and Transformation at Allianz Life.

Education, certifications, and memberships:

  • Member of the ATA Board of Directors
  • Member of the COO Community
  • Degree in IT Science from Costanza Di Milano (Italy)
  • Board member of the Minnesota Council on Economic Education
