Lorinda Niemeyer

Head of Sustainability
Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Focused on:

  • Sustainability strategy
  • Sustainability reporting
  • Climate change and risk management

Background:

With more than 25 years of experience in the areas of sustainability and strategy, Niemeyer offers deep insights into the role sustainability plays in managing businesses for the long term and in translating the changing environmental and social landscape into new opportunities.

Experience:

  • At Allianz Life, Niemeyer leads sustainability strategy, leveraging our position as a leading insurer, investor, and employer to shape a more secure future for our customers, business, and communities.
  • Extensive experience incorporating sustainability trends and insights into customer experience, product development, and brand awareness.
  • Over 20 years of consulting experience, including managing director and head of thought leadership at a boutique firm, as well as the leader of internal sustainability efforts at McKinsey & Company.
  • Spent six years as a Special Agent with the U.S. government, holding positions at both the Environmental Protection Agency and the Department of Defense.

Education, certifications, and memberships:

  • MBA from Stanford University Graduate School of Business
  • M.A. in education policy and administration from Stanford University
  • B.A. and M.S. from University of Washington (Phi Beta Kappa)
  • Global Association of Risk Professionals, Sustainability and Climate Risk (SCR®) Certificate
