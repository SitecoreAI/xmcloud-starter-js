Jasmine Jirele

President and Chief Executive Officer
Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Background:

With more than 25 years in the financial services industry, Jirele has broad experience in financial strategy, marketing, product, and operations.

Experience:

  • More than 15 years with Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America (Allianz Life), focused on delivering innovative retirement solutions to financial professionals and their clients.
  • Director of Allianz Australia Life Insurance (Holdings, Limited, and Policy Services).
  • Prior experience as executive vice president at Wells Fargo.

Education, certifications, and memberships:

  • B.A. in business and journalism from the University of St. Thomas (Minnesota)
  • MBA from Hamline University (Minnesota)
  • FINRA Series 7 and 24 securities registrations
  • Chair Elect and Executive Committee member of the American Council of Life Insurers (ACLI)
  • Member of the Board of Trustees of Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota, and member of the Finance and Governance Committees
  • Member of the Board of the U.S. Chamber of Commerce
  • Member of the College Possible National Board of Directors and Finance Committee
