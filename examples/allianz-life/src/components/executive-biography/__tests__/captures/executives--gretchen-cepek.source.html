Gretchen Cepek

Chief Legal Officer
Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Background:

As chief legal officer, Cepek leads all of the company’s legal matters, serving the best interests of the company, its customers, and its distribution. She oversees legal litigation, corporate governance, special investigations, regulatory matters, ethics, compliance, and government relations. She is also a member of the company's Executive Leadership Group.

Experience:

  • Started at Allianz Life in 2009 as vice president in the corporate legal department, responsible for government relations and for supervising a team of lawyers who specialize in developing new products and who provide legal guidance to Allianz Life in the areas of distribution and operations.
  • Member of the Allianz Global Inclusion Council, co-executive sponsor of the Allianz Global Race and Cultural Ethnicity employee network, and former sponsor of the Allianz Life Asian American Pacific Islander employee resource group.
  • Serves as the chair of the Allianz Foundation of North America board.
  • Previously provided strategic legal counsel for Woodbury Financial Services, a retail broker/dealer.
  • Previously was a partner at Querrey & Harrow in Chicago, specializing in commercial litigation.

Education, certifications, and memberships:

  • B.A. in political science and history from Illinois Wesleyan University
  • Juris doctor degree from Valparaiso University School of Law (Indiana)
  • Board member of the Twin Cities Diversity in Practice
  • Board member of Southern Minnesota Regional Legal Services
  • President of the Life Insurance Council of New York board of directors
  • Advisory Council of the American College Maguire Center for Ethics in Financial Services
  • Former board chair of the Minnesota chapter of the Make-A-Wish Foundation
  • Former president of the Association of Life Insurance Counsel
  • Former board member of the Alzheimer’s Association of Minnesota-North Dakota
