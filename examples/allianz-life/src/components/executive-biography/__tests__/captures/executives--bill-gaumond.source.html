Bill Gaumond

Chief Financial Officer
Allianz Life Insurance Company of North America

Background:

With nearly 30 years in the financial services industry, Gaumond has broad experience with financial planning, financial management, financial reporting, data and analytics, and corporate risk management.

Experience:

  • 10 years as Chief Financial Officer for Allianz Life.
  • Over 20 years at Allianz Life in roles including senior vice president of the Asset Liability Management and Investment Risk Management department.
  • Two years as a senior member of the Group Planning and Controlling department for Allianz SE in Munich, Germany.
  • Nine years at PricewaterhouseCoopers.

Education, certifications, and memberships:

  • Member of the board of governors for the Hill-Murray Foundation
  • Member of the board of directors for Bolder Options in Minneapolis, Minnesota
  • Former member of the LL Global Board of Directors for LIMRA and LOMA
  • Bachelor of Business Administration degree in accounting from the University of Notre Dame (Indiana); graduated summa cum laude
