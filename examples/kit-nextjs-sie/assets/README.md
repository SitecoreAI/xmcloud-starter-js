## Art-direction rules

- Favor warm Texas daylight, golden-hour amber, terracotta, cream, limestone, walnut, olive green, and charcoal.
- Keep blue largely limited to realistic sky or a natural gas flame.
- Use authentic homes, communities, utility equipment, and PPE. Avoid futuristic infrastructure, exaggerated flames, or staged stock-photo gestures.
- Preserve clear negative space where the design places orange or charcoal copy panels over an image.
- Do not generate words, logos, account numbers, meter labels, branded uniforms, or readable screen content.
- Use the official light logo in the header and the reversed logo in the charcoal footer. Do not ask an image model to reproduce either mark.
