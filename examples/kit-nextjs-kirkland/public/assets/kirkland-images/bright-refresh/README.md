# Kirkland daylight image refresh

This folder contains 13 direct replacement images for the homepage, article
pages, and location pages, plus one optional News and Insights landing-page
image. All files are original AI-generated demonstration assets. They do not
reproduce photographs from kirkland.com, and they contain no Kirkland logo or
wordmark.

Every image is a 1536 x 1024 RGB PNG. The important subject matter is kept
within a central crop-safe area for the site's 16:9 cards and article images,
30:19 page headers, and responsive `object-cover` treatments.

## Generation direction

All images were generated as new photorealistic editorial scenes, not as edits
of the previous files. The shared direction was premium global-law-firm
photography with clear daytime weather, warm natural light, pale stone, ivory,
light oak, restrained blue accents, realistic architecture, and responsive
crop safety.

Per-image scene briefs:

- Homepage hero: bright law-office pavilion on the right, with clean blue-sky
  copy space on the left.
- One firm. No headquarters.: integrated global legal team collaborating around
  a round table in a bright, panoramic office.
- Transactional: immaculate daylight boardroom prepared for a consequential
  transaction.
- Litigation: dignified pale-stone courthouse interior beneath a skylight.
- Restructuring: constructive, sunlit strategy room with abstract planning
  materials.
- Intellectual Property: luminous precision glass, optics, and engineered
  prototypes.
- BlackRock and Meta article: landscaped hyperscale data center campus under a
  clear blue sky.
- PAI and Pasubio article: orderly Italian leather atelier in warm daylight.
- UK NSI article: sunlit civic stone architecture opening toward modern London.
- Locations landing: globally styled rooftop pavilion and broad daytime city
  panorama.
- Houston: downtown skyline from a bright terrace with restrained Texas
  planting.
- London: historic masonry and modern City towers in crisp spring light.
- New York: Midtown skyline and the Empire State Building in clear late-morning
  light.
- Optional News and Insights landing: airy legal research library and editorial
  worktable.

Every prompt excluded rain, wet surfaces, overcast skies, dusk, night, gloomy
exposure, readable text, logos, watermarks, flags, and generic legal imagery
such as gavels, handshakes, and scales of justice.
