You may need UK national security approval if your deal is a mandatory notification under the National Security and Investment Act Guidance, meaning it involves a sensitive sector, passes control thresholds, and must be cleared pre-completion. Kirkland & Ellis LLP

Mandatory vs. Voluntary Rules

  • Mandatory sectors (17 areas): Includes artificial intelligence, defence, critical suppliers, energy, transport, and quantum technologies. The National Law Review
  • Control thresholds: Triggered when acquiring more than 25%, 50%, or 75% of voting rights/shares, or gaining material influence over policy. Norton Rose Fulbright
  • Voluntary notification may be advisable for other qualifying acquisitions where national security concerns could arise.
